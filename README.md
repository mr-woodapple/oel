oel is a beer library - the one you need, trust me!

oel allows you to:

1. Add beers, the brewery behind them, basic details and an image of the beer.
1. Log beers, saving what beer you had, how it was served, where you had it, give it a rating and store an image of the situation.
1. See a map of where you had your beers.


## But why?

Fair question: Well when I was on holiday in Sweden, I always end up in their "Systembolaget" stores where they sell beers - and I always forget which one I liked. And this time, I decided to do something against that: oel.

> Fun fact: "Oel" is the swedish word for beer. Now you know! :)

## Get started

oel is available for self hosting through a Docker image published on every new release. There's a template `compose.yaml` file in the root of this repo, you may use it as a starting point.
